# CultureTechLens Corrections

CultureTechLens maintains this file to document substantive corrections to published research.

## Correction principles

When a correction is necessary, CTL aims to:

1. identify the affected research or publication;
2. describe what was incorrect or incomplete;
3. provide the corrected information;
4. identify the supporting evidence;
5. record the date of the correction;
6. preserve relevant version history when transparency requires it.
