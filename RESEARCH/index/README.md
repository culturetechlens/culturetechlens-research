# The CultureTechLens Index — Research Record

**CULTURETECHLENS · "Culture, Clearly Seen." · Black Cultural Intelligence**

The monthly, data-driven ranking of cultural figures, moments, artifacts, or places drawn from Black Chicago's cultural record. Each edition ranks one category per month on a transparent, reproducible 0–100 score.

## Files

- [CTL-IDX-001-methodology.md](CTL-IDX-001-methodology.md) — program methodology, version 1.1 (2026-09-28). Status: finalized; the edition-level scoring procedure is fully specified here.
- [editions/2026-10/index-scores.csv](editions/2026-10/index-scores.csv) — Edition 2026-10 (Artifacts) raw scores: rank, name, category, composite score, attention / evidence / momentum components, 30-day pageviews. 10 subjects.
- [Published deposit](https://zenodo.org/records/23081630) — Edition 2026-10 deposited on Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23081630, CC BY 4.0.

## What the Index measures

Cultural resonance — how present a subject is in the documented record and in current public attention — not worth, greatness, or moral standing. Rankings describe the data. They do not crown winners.

## Cadence

Published monthly on the first Tuesday of the month, rotating categories: Artifacts → Moments → Places → Figures → repeat.
