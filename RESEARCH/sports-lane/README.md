## Dossier format

Every dossier follows the same structure:

1. **The verified record** — names, dates, events, with named sources
2. **The mechanism** — economics, barriers, or press tactics, depending on the subject
3. **What it cost them** — the second half of the flagship question
4. **The Chicago connection** — the city's buildings, teams, and streets in the story
5. **Candy hooks** — short-form angles for video (the names earn the click; the evidence is the medicine)
6. **Image notes** — rights-cleared photography leads with license status
7. **Gaps** — what could not be verified, stated plainly, never smoothed over

## Evidence grades

Every factual claim carries a grade, per CTL methodology (see `METHODOLOGY.md`):

- **VERIFIED** — confirmed by two or more independent sources
- **SUPPORTED** — confirmed by one credible source, consistent with the record
- **PROVISIONAL** — plausible, single-source or partially confirmed
- **DISPUTED** — sources conflict; both accounts stated
- **UNRESOLVED** — could not be verified; listed in Gaps, never asserted
