# CTL Index Promotion Playbook

**The evergreen flagship:** the CTL 10 Index — the monthly ranking people wait for. William's institution test, applied: CTL counts when the Index is the thing others have to cite. Every edition gets the full drumbeat. No edition ships quietly.

*Draft 2026-10-05. For William's review — not yet adopted.*

---

## The monthly drumbeat (edition day = first Tuesday, 6 AM CT)

Run in order. Each step feeds the next.

1. **Site publish** — the edition page goes live with charts, scored table, methodology link, limitations, and corrections log. The CSV downloads. This is the canonical record everything else points to.
2. **Zenodo deposit** — the edition dataset + release notes deposited the same day, DOI minted, CC BY 4.0, open access. The DOI is the citable handle; the site page is the readable front.
3. **Newsletter feature** — the edition leads the next Substack send: top 3 subjects, the signature finding (the one number worth remembering), link to the full page. Subject line carries the edition topic, not the brand.
4. **Video** — the 90-second Short ships within 48 hours (hook + signature finding + DOI card); the long-form deep dive (8–12 min, chaptered) ships within the week. Both point at the edition page.
5. **Five media pitches** — from the existing pitch list, five targets per edition, each with a one-line news hook drawn from the edition's actual findings (not the brand story). Log every pitch in the CRM: who, when, hook, response.
6. **Social cutdowns** — one data card per top-5 subject (chart + one sentence), posted across X, Threads, Instagram, Pinterest through the week. Each card links the edition page.
7. **Pin for the month** — the edition page is pinned on X and the Short is pinned on YouTube until the next edition drops. The flagship is always one click away.

## The always-on layer

Things that promote every edition without anyone remembering:

- **Homepage feature slot** — the current edition sits in the homepage hero rotation until replaced.
- **/start-here/ door** — "See what we rank" points at the Index hub, always current.
- **Press-kit fast facts** — /press/ carries the latest edition's signature finding and DOI (updated monthly, same day as publish).
- **Email signature line** — every outbound email from info@ carries: *This month's CTL 10: [topic] — culturetechlens.com/research/culturetechlens-index/* (updated monthly).

## What "actively promoted" means, measurably

An edition counts as promoted when all of these are true:

- **3+ citations/month** — the edition DOI or page cited in papers, articles, syllabi, or substantive posts. Tracked via the press watcher + Scholar alerts.
- **1+ media mention/edition** — a journalist, newsletter, or outlet references the edition by name. Logged in the CRM.
- **5 pitches sent** — the floor, not the ceiling. No edition gets fewer.
- **Video shipped** — the Short at minimum. An edition with no video is an unpublished edition.

Review quarterly: which hooks landed, which targets responded, what the citation curve looks like. Kill what doesn't move; double what does.

## What this playbook does NOT do

- No paid promotion until the Ad Grants / paid-test question is settled (separate decision).
- No pitch without a real finding — the hook comes from the edition's data, never from brand adjectives.
- No metric gaming — pageviews are reported, never purchased; citations are earned, never solicited from friendly pens.
