## 11. Verdict

**What the evidence establishes (VERIFIED):** Joyner's training, salon, patent, and Walker career; Overton's Chicago move and the 1922 building; Johnson Products' founding, AMEX listing, Soul Train sponsorship, and Johnson's death; Soft Sheen's founding and 1998 sale; Freeman's barbershop (1942); the FSA-OWI 1941 documentation; Poro College's 1918 founding.

**What the evidence suggests (SUPPORTED):** the training pipeline's scale; the salon-manufacturer closed loop; the barbershop/beauty-parlor as political space (national scholarship); the displacement-and-consolidation arc; the 1993 sale's price and context.

**What remains disputed:** Joyner's death date; the 1983 voter-drive numbers; Joyner's cosmetology-law date.

**What remains unknown:** nearly everything at the level of the ordinary named shop — names, addresses, revenues, margins, survival. This is not a footnote; it is the finding.

**What CTL should research next:** Defender ad/mention index (1915–1975); Polk directory sweep; Illinois licensing legislative history; oral-history listening with claims encoded; Joyner papers at the Harsh Collection; NMAH object records. (Full list in §22.)

**Release judgment:** Fit for Research status. Not fit for "Published" until the ordinary-shop layer (ECON-001's directory work) or the open verification items are resolved. The uncertainty is visible and load-bearing — that is the point.
