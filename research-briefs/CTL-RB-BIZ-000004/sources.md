## Government and institutional records
- **U.S. Congress — Congressional Record, Vol. 151, Issue 57 (2005), "Chicago Defender Centennial."** Statement of ~300 first-run copies and >250,000 weekly sales by 1929. https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-151/issue-57/house-section/article/H2948-4
- **National Park Service — National Register nomination (NRHP 64000173), Chicago Defender Building text.** Documents 1920 start of occupancy at 3435 S. Indiana Ave. https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64000173_text
- **Encyclopedia of Chicago (Chicago History Museum).** *Chicago Defender* founding May 5, 1905; ~120,000 circulation late 1910s (a third Chicago, two-thirds elsewhere, ~80,000 Southern). https://www.encyclopedia.chicagohistory.org/pages/283.html
- **Library of Congress, FSA–OWI Collection.** Jack Delano negatives: newsboy selling the *Chicago Defender* (LC-USW3-002867-D, April 1942); John H. Sengstacke portrait (LC-USW3-003011-E, March 1942). No known restrictions on publication. https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2017833086/ (newsboy)
- **Grolier Club exhibition catalog — "One Hundred Books Famous in Typography / American Magazines: African American Magazines."** *Ebony*: 25,000 first issue sold out; 325,000 circulation by 1947. https://grolierclub.omeka.net/exhibits/show/american-magazines/am-african-american-magazines
- **NYPL Research Catalog — The HistoryMakers video oral history with John H. Johnson (recorded Nov. 11, 2004, Chicago; 11 video files, ~5h20m).** https://www.nypl.org/research/research-catalog/bib/b22367300
- **The HistoryMakers — Finding aid A2004_231 (John H. Johnson interview).** $500 loan + $6,000 charter subscriptions; Negro Digest to Black World; *Ebony* 1945; *Jet* 1951. https://www.thehistorymakers.org/sites/default/files/A2004_231_EAD.pdf
- **Britannica — Bud Billiken Parade.** Parade scale ranking. https://www.britannica.com/art/Bud-Billiken-Parade
- **Zinn Education Project — "The Black Press: Soldiers Without Swords"** (distributor trailer link; film context). https://www.zinnedproject.org/materials/soldiers-without-swords/

## Trade and industry journalism
- **Editor & Publisher (1999), "Death Tax Claims Storied Tabloid."** Sengstacke 70% ownership; trust sale requirement; ~$3M estate-tax bill; ~230,000 peak national circulation; 13,786 weekday / 16,268 weekend ABC circulation; believed losing money. https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/death-tax-claims-storied-tabloid,100415
- **Editor & Publisher (2002), "Chicago Defender Sale OK'd."** $8.5M for 91% plus $2.4M debt assumption ($10.9M deal). https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/chicago-defender-sale-okd,104472
- **Editor & Publisher (2003), "Judge OKs Sale of Chicago Defender."** $1.5M technology-investment commitment. https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/judge-oks-sale-of-chicago-defender,22394
- **Editor & Publisher (2002), "The Revival of the Black Press in America."** 14,629 daily ABC circulation (2002); January 2003 purchase described as $8.1M. https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/the-revival-of-the-black-press-in-america,27852
- **Columbia Journalism Review — "The Not-So-Great Migration."** *Defender* 257,000 (1945) → 33,000 (1970); *Courier* 202,000 → 20,000. https://www.cjr.org/feature/the_not-so-great_migration.php
- **Mediapost (2016), "Johnson Publishing Sells Ebony, Jet."** *Ebony* audited circulation −22% (1.51M H1 2005 → 1.18M H1 2015); sale to Clear View at undisclosed price with assumed debt. https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/278190/johnson-publishing-sells-ebony-jet.html
