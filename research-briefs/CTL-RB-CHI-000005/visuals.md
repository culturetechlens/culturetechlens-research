## Data-driven graphics (build at publish)

### V-GRAPH-1 — Black population of Chicago, 1910–1970
- Bar/line chart of decennial figures: 44,103 → 109,458 → 233,903 → ~278,000 →
  492,265 → ~813,000 → 1,102,620, with Black share of city population.
- **Build only after exact Census table provenance is located** (brief §22 rule);
  until then the chart carries a "figures pending table verification" flag.

### V-GRAPH-2 — First-wave settlement map (c. 1910–1940)
- Census-tract choropleth of Black population share for 1910, 1920, 1940, with the
  three boundary descriptions overlaid: White 1919 (railroad–30th–40th–Ellis),
  Drake & Cayton 1920s (State–31st–Cottage Grove–47th), 1940s (Cermak–Lake
  Michigan–South Chicago–LaSalle).
- Sources: NHGIS tract data; CULtivate census maps.

### V-GRAPH-3 — Second-wave settlement map (c. 1940–1970)
- Census-tract choropleth for 1940, 1950, 1960, 1970 showing southward expansion
  (past 63rd, 75th, 90th) and West Side concentrations (North Lawndale et al.),
  with CHA project sites marked.
- Sources: NHGIS tract data; CULtivate maps; CHA project locations.

### V-GRAPH-4 — Constraint/enabler causal diagram
- Two-column flow diagram: five enablers (IC railroad; chain migration; Olivet/YMCA;
  Black press; end of covenant enforceability) and five constraints (covenants;
  violence; lending discrimination; predatory tenure; state siting policy), with
  arrows showing how constraints adapted after 1948 (law → markets → state action).
