## Scholarship
- **Joshua Clark Davis, "For the Records: How African American Consumers and Music Retailers Created Commercial Public Space in the 1960s and 1970s South," *Southern Cultures* 17, no. 4 (Winter 2011), 71–90.** — full article. - **Thomas Calkins, "More than Sound: Record Stores in Majority Black Neighborhoods in Chicago, Milwaukee, and Detroit, 1970–2010," *City & Community* 18, no. 3 (September 2019), 853–873, DOI 10.1111/cico.12433.** — city-directory/census-tract dataset, 1970–2010. → LIFE-010, LIFE-011 context. *Note: a Sci-Hub mirror surfaced during discovery; it must never be cited. Cite the DOI/bibliographic record only.*
