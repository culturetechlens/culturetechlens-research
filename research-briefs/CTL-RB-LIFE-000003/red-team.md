# Red Team — CTL-RB-LIFE-000003: The Family Reunion as Cultural Infrastructure

Compiled 2026-09-28. Purpose: attack the brief's reasoning, sourcing, and presentation before publication. Each audit states the risk, what was done, and what remains open.

## 1. Functionalism audit

**Risk:** Treating every reunion activity as "serving" the institution — reading purpose into practice, and practice into necessity.
**What was done:** The seven functions (§§6–12) are each tied to documentary or scholarly evidence, not to inference from festivity. Counter-evidence is retained inside the brief: Copeland's failure modes (limited capital, weak recordkeeping) show the institution malfunctioning [C004]; mutual-aid claims are held at PROVISIONAL on thin evidence rather than promoted [C023]; no outcome claims (health, well-being) are made [C025].
**Open:** The brief still frames through function; a rival framing (reunion as consumption event, as status competition, as obligation resented by attendees) has almost no literature in the source base. Absence of that literature is noted, not filled.

## 2. Anecdote audit

**Risk:** Letting vivid single cases stand in for the typical.
**What was done:** The Doswell trees (extraordinary: 5,000+ names, three museum holdings) are explicitly flagged as one family's archive, "not the typical reunion" [C014 note; C022 note]. The 2016 minutes (ordinary: dues, balance, scholarships) are presented as documentary evidence of common structures, corroborated by two independent sources [C001]. Single-family cases (Thompson 2001; the Smithsonian Mag family) are labeled as such [C010][C022].
**Open:** With no prevalence data [C024], "typical" remains an informed judgment, not a measurement.

## 3. Selection and survivorship bias

**Risk:** The documented reunions are the organized, successful, record-keeping ones. Failed, lapsed, conflict-torn, and tiny reunions leave no minutes and get no op-eds.
**What was done:** Named in the brief (§22) and in the research agenda (§23: "commission oral histories… with attention to… reunions that lapsed"). The 1993 literature's "reclamation committee" hints at lapsed-kin recovery, but the hint is not inflated [C001].
**Open:** The brief cannot describe the reunion that died. That is a structural limit of the archive, stated as such.

## 4. Celebrity bias

**Risk:** Substituting famous families for ordinary life.
**What was done:** None. Every case is an ordinary family (Felix/Blackwell, Thompson, Allen, Perryman). The NCNW celebration — the one celebrity-adjacent entity — is used only as a boundary marker, not as evidence [C017].
**Open:** None identified.

## 5. Missing women and institutions

**Risk:** Under-crediting women's labor; over-centering or under-centering adjacent institutions (church, funeral, civic).
**What was done:** Women's genealogical and organizing labor is documented (Doswell, Gibbs, quoted planners; the field's scholars are women) but the brief refuses to quantify what it cannot measure — "women do most of the organizing" is not stated as a verified general fact [C016]. Churches are placed adjacent (committees, venues) without being mistaken for governors [§17]. Funerals appear once, as documented origin (1953), not as theory [C015].
**Open:** Systematic gender data on reunion organizing does not exist in the located literature; oral-history follow-up is specified (§23).

## 6. Weak visual provenance

**Risk:** Illustrating the brief with images that don't show what they claim, or whose rights don't allow use.
**What was done:** No historical photograph of a Black family reunion met the rights bar in this pass, so none was used. Sourced items are reference-linked with full provenance and explicit rights status (V01, V02), never downloaded without clearance. Rejected candidates are documented with reasons (Teenie Harris/CMOA rights restriction; private-family photos; LOC photos of white gatherings; unverifiable titles). Built graphics carry exact data and chart types instead of decoration. Video selections are institutional channels only, assessed via title/description/channel authority, never presented as watched.
**Open:** Embed status and upload dates for all four videos are unverified — re-check at publication per the link-rot plan. The Perryman photo's rights remain unverified; it stays link-only.

## 7. Copyright

**Risk:** Reproducing protected material.
**What was done:** The 2016 minutes are quoted as facts, not reproduced. The Perryman photograph is cited, not downloaded. No CMOA/Teenie Harris image was downloaded (rights restricted). No YouTube video was downloaded or re-uploaded (embed/link only). All built graphics are original.
**Open:** None identified, pending publication-time re-checks.

## 8. Entity duplication

**Risk:** The same person/institution appearing under variant names across entities.json, relationships.json, and the brief.
**What was done:** Entities validated: 37 entities, 13 edges, no dangling references, no self-loops. Spot checks: "Ione D. Vargus" / "Vargus" consistent; "National Museum of African American History and Culture" / "NMAAHC" mapped to ENT-I02; "Thelma Doswell" / "Thelma Short Doswell" mapped to ENT-P06; "Black Family Reunion Celebration" (NCNW) distinguished from "family reunion" (generic) throughout.
**Open:** "Allen Family Reunion" (the 2016 minutes' family) vs. "Laura L. Allen" (scholar) — distinct entities; the brief never conflates them, but a future knowledge-graph ingest should guard the name collision explicitly.

## 9. Nostalgia check

**Risk:** The brief's enemy per its own mandate: sentiment substituting for evidence.
**What was done:** Adjectives of warmth were struck in favor of mechanisms (dues, minutes, committees, trees). The closing section (§25) states the institution's dignity as a conclusion from evidence, not as a premise. Uncertainties (§21) are given equal typographic weight to findings.
**Open:** Reader reception can't be controlled; the claim ledger is the structural defense.

## 10. Largest residual risk

The Chicago-to-South reunion pattern [C007] — the brief's geographic thesis — is UNRESOLVED. If published with the current evidence, the brief must keep its Chicago claims inside the documented planning/market evidence [C013][C027] and mark the travel pattern as a research target, which it does. The failure mode to avoid: letting the map graphic (V03) imply what the text denies. V03 carries the uncertainty callout by design.
