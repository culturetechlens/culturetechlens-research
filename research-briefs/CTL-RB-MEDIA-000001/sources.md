# CTL-RB-MEDIA-000001 — Sources

**CULTURETECHLENS · Culture, Clearly Seen. · Black Cultural Intelligence**

All sources accessed 2026-09-28 unless noted. Reliability notes are honest: commemorative, attributed, tertiary, and abstract-level sources are labeled as such.

## Primary / government records

- **S13 — FBI file, "The Chicago Defender."** Internet Archive item `TheChicagoDefender`; creator Federal Bureau of Investigation; marked public-domain (CC Public Domain Mark 1.0). 3-page file; 1964-10-14 memo on the C. Sumner Stone firing read in full. https://archive.org/details/TheChicagoDefender
- **S02 — Congressional Record, Vol. 143, No. 74, tribute to John H. Sengstacke.** States Pullman porters were "unofficial circulation agents" dropping Defender copies at barbershops and churches along Southern runs; cites up to 200,000 weekly copies during WWII. Commemorative speech — a government record, not business documentation. https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-143/issue-74/house-section/article/H3248-2
- **S03 — Congressional Record, Vol. 151, No. 57, Defender centennial tribute.** Reports more than 250,000 copies weekly by 1929. Commemorative/secondary. https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-151/issue-57/house-section/article/H2948-4
- **S11 — Illinois House resolution (c.1920).** Cited 23,000 Defender copies sold weekly in New York City. Commemorative character; accessed via prior research (BPM-003 chain).
- **Scott, Emmett J. *Negro Migration During the War* (1920).** Federal report documenting Southern reception of the Defender, including the Mississippi "hungry mule" observation. Quoted via S04; full text a priority target (§24 of brief).

## Journalism & trade press

- **S04 — New Journal and Guide, "Letters to Black Press Reveal Influence in Migration Movement."** Quotes 1916–1917 Defender reader letters (Jacksonville FL, Lutcher LA, Bessemer AL, New Orleans, Memphis) and the Sept 2, 1916 Savannah migrants front page. https://thenewjournalandguide.com/letters-to-black-press-reveal-influence-in-migration-movement/
- **S05 — Editor & Publisher, "Four Years After Last Audit, Chicago Defender Shows Modest Circ Uptick."** USPS statement: 15,534 average paid circulation (2007); last ABC audit (6 mo. ending 3/31/2003): 14,479 weekday / 17,204 weekend; resigned ABC late 2004. Contemporary trade reporting. https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/four-years-after-last-audit-chicago-defender-shows-modest-circ-uptick,126612
- **S06 — Editor & Publisher, "Chicago Defender, Long a Web Holdout, to Launch a Podcast."** Website launched December 2004; centennial podcast a first for a Black-oriented newspaper. https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/chicago-defender-long-a-web-holdout-to-launch-a-podcast,111946
- **S07 — Smithsonian Magazine, "Chicago Defender, Iconic Black Newspaper, Releasing Its Last Print Issue" (2019).** Print ~16,000 vs ~475,000 monthly unique website visitors. Reputable journalism citing the paper. https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/chicago-defender-iconic-black-newspaper-releasing-its-last-print-issue-180972586/
- **S08 — Post News Group / NNPA, "Chicago Defender Newspaper Moves to Digital-Only With Its July 11 Edition" (2019).** Publisher Hiram Jackson: website readership several hundred thousand, Facebook 200,000+, print under 20,000 weekly. Attributed publisher figures. https://www.postnewsgroup.com/chicago-defender-newspaper-moves-to-digital-only-with-its-july-11-edition/
- **S10 — Chicago Reporter, "Rodgers News Stand."** Bronzeville newsstand (bought 1945) selling the Defender alongside the Tribune and Sun-Times. Reputable local journalism. http://www.chicagoreporter.com/rodgers-news-stand/
