# Red Team — CTL-RB-MEDIA-000002 "WVON and Black Chicago"

Adversarial review of the brief's claims, per the CTL red-team protocol: anachronisms, ownership/date conflicts, nostalgia-driven overstatement, missing women and rank-and-file staff, circular station lore, copyright.

## 1. Anachronisms — checked against the verified timeline

- **The 1983 anachronism (killed):** Any sentence implying an all-talk WVON powered Harold Washington's 1983 first campaign is false. Talk began March 1986 (Muwakkil 1989 quoting GM Hoyett Owens; HR0201; Searcy). The brief uses only the timeline-compatible framing: 1983 = music-plus-information/commentary with Lu Palmer's "We Shall See in '83"; 1987 = all-talk environment. (Claim D01.)
- **The January 1989 "talk debut" (killed):** belongs to WGCI-AM's competing Black-talk format, not WVON. Not used anywhere in the brief.
- **"Voice of the Nation" (contained):** The re-glossing belongs to Midway's 1984 call-letter reclamation (Searcy), not the Chess era. The brief never applies it to 1963–1983.
- **1690 ownership language (contained):** The brief never says Midway "owns" 1690. Licensee/owner is iHeartMedia (Clear Channel); Midway operates/programs under the LMA.
- **Chess acquisition date (flagged, not smoothed):** Searcy says spring 1962; Broadcasting's sale announcement is February 1963. The brief marks this UNRESOLVED (U01) rather than picking the cleaner date.

## 2. Ownership/date conflicts — resolved or flagged

- Midway founding: 1977 (Searcy) vs. 1979 (industry trail). The brief presents both with attribution (D03) and treats WXOL's 1979 on-air launch as the firm date.
- Call-letter return: oral histories say 1983; FCC-compatible date is March 28, 1984. The brief uses 1984 and documents the variance (V06).
- Combined/Gannett: HR0201 compresses to "Gannett 1977"; the brief gives the two-step (Combined 1977 → Gannett merger 1978) as SUPPORTED (V15).

## 3. Nostalgia-driven overstatement — quarantined

- **"Consistently top 5"** (HR0201): kept as a repeated official claim (S17), not upgraded; primary ratings books were not consulted. "Top 3" variants excluded.
- **Motown-first arrangement:** supported via Searcy's citation chain to the documentary *The Dawn of Urban Radio* (S01); presented as scholarly-sourced, not audited business fact.
- **"First Black-talk format" / "first station designed for Black Chicago":** kept as resolution-level/commemorative claims with explicit qualification (S23, P04); competing stations (WYNR, WAAF, WSBC, WBEE) named as the reason.
- **Loudspeaker trucks, Jesse Jackson's assassination call-in, "bully pulpit":** all tribute-only; graded PROVISIONAL and excluded from the relationship graph (P01–P03). The brief documents what Searcy *does* support (24-hour coverage, Wood to Atlanta, headlights, community calm) and stops there.
- **2022 Spann tribute's "purchased WVON on April 1, 1963":** marked DISPUTED (D02); the brief states the correct chain (Chess → Globetrotter → Gannett → Midway 1984).
