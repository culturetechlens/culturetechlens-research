# CTL-RB-MEDIA-000003 — Visuals Plan

**Brief:** Black Images on Chicago Television · **Status:** Research (v0.1)

## Visual strategy

Most early Chicago local broadcasts were never preserved; no rights-cleared archival footage of the era's landmark programs could be sourced. The plan therefore anchors on **two sourced historical portraits** (Don Cornelius; the Staple Singers on *Soul Train*), **two CTL-built data graphics** (the era × dimensions map; the dimensions chart), and **three link-only videos** — all rights-sensitive broadcast clips are *link-don't-embed*, per the media-specific caution.

**Count:** 2 sourced · 2 build-at-publish · 1 unavailable-with-reason · 3 videos (link-only)

## V-3 — Build at publish: Era-by-era representation map (anchor graphic)

- **What:** Data-driven SVG (CTL-branded: black / deep red / gold), one lane per representation dimension (on-air performance · news presence · creative control · ownership · advertising images) across the four eras (1947–1967 · 1968–1979 · 1980–1999 · 2000–present), plotting the brief's dated milestones (Star Quest 1947; Jubilee Showcase 1963–84; Our People 1968; Bird of the Iron Feather 1970; Soul Train 1970/1971; Porterfield anchor 1978/boycott 1985; Burrell 1971; WJYS 1991).
- **Data source:** `claim-ledger.json` (SUPPORTED and VERIFIED claims only; every plotted point keyed to a claim ID).
- **Status:** `build-at-publish`
- **Build spec:** read claim ledger → select dated claims with grade ≥ SUPPORTED → one SVG lane per dimension → CTL visual identity; embed claim IDs as data attributes for the interactive edition.

## V-4 — Build at publish: Representation-dimensions matrix (chart)

- **What:** Data-driven SVG chart: qualitative presence/absence matrix — five representation dimensions × four eras — shaded cells with per-cell claim-ID references; labeled "interpretive summary" with a visible note that no systematic content sample underlies the scoring.
- **Data source:** `claim-ledger.json`, same grading floor as V-3.
- **Status:** `build-at-publish`
- **Build spec:** matrix from era-boundary claims (C-ERA-1A … C-ERA-4B) → CTL-branded SVG; uncertainty cells flagged (e.g., advertising-images cell for Era 1 marked UNRESOLVED).

## V-5 — Unavailable with reason: Archival stills of *Our People* / *Bird of the Iron Feather*

- **Status:** `unavailable-with-reason`
- **Reason:** No rights-cleared photographs or footage of *Our People* (WTTW, 1968–) or *Bird of the Iron Feather* (WTTW/NET, 1970) were located in public sources. Broadcast stills and screenshots require item-specific fair-use analysis; none was performed for this research-stage brief. WTTW archive materials and the Museum of Broadcast Communications' holdings were not yet inventoried (see §22 Further Research). The Smithsonian's *Soul Train*-era objects (neon sign, Scramble Board, costumes) postdate the Chicago period and are restricted ("permission required / unknown — restrictions possible"), so they are not substituted.
