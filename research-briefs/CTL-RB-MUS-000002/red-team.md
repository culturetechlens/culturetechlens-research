## Verdict

### WHAT THE EVIDENCE ESTABLISHES
- The Warehouse at 206 S. Jefferson (1977–82 period of significance) is the club from which "house music" took its name; Frankie Knuckles was resident from March 1977 before a Black, Latino, largely gay crowd (city landmark report).
- Larry Levan declined the Warehouse residency and recommended Knuckles; the two had developed their DJ practice at the Continental Baths (city landmark report).
- Robert Williams founded/promoted the Warehouse, rebranded it as the Music Box in 1983 with Ron Hardy resident, while Knuckles opened the Power Plant at 1015 N. Halsted (city landmark report).
- The Hot Mix 5's WBMX broadcasts (from 1981) and dance-music-only retail (Imports Etc., Gramaphone) were the diffusion infrastructure.
- "On & On" (Jes Say, January 1984, Saunders + Lawrence) is the first house track committed to vinyl; "Your Love" circulated on cassette first; "Acid Tracks" was club-tested before its 1987 pressing.
- Disco Demolition Night happened July 12, 1979; it did not cause house.

### WHAT THE EVIDENCE SUGGESTS
- House was a collective creation: no single inventor; the three-births model (club culture → DJ practice → recorded genre) fits the documented sequence.
- Cassette circulation was the proto-distribution system that preceded pressing.
- Cheap second-hand machines (TB-303, TR-707/727) were the material precondition of the 1985–87 sound.
- "House music is disco's revenge" captures the scene's self-understanding (attributed, not proven).

### WHAT REMAINS DISPUTED OR UNKNOWN
- Every "first record" claim, unless criterion-specified.
- The Music Box's address; the Warehouse's founding-year narrative; Ron Hardy's death year.
- Trax's founding year/credits and the catalog's royalty title chain (2022 lawsuit allegations unadjudicated).
- The acid-name etymology; TB-303 production-run figures.
- The women, workers, and dancers who made the scene — a research emergency.

2. **Oral-history capture (urgent):** surviving DJs, dancers, club workers, and women participants from the Warehouse/Music Box/Power Plant eras; Detroit figures on the Imports Etc. bridge.
3. **Catalog-rights research:** Trax chain of title and the 2022 lawsuit outcome; "Your Love" credit documentation.
