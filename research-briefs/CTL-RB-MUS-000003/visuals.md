Discipline: no storefront, portrait, advertisement, or price-list image is

### I-1 (image). Maxwell Street street-market ecology — SOURCED

- **Type:** archival photograph (sourced)
- **Status:** sourced
- **Creator:** Russell Lee, Farm Security Administration / Office of War
  Information
- **Date:** April 1941
- **Title:** "Corner of Maxwell and Halsted Streets, Chicago"
- **Source institution:** Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division,
  FSA-OWI Collection
- **Reproduction number:** LC-USF33-012984-M5
- **Rights:** Public domain (U.S. federal government work). LOC rights
  advisory for FSA-OWI stills: no known restrictions on publication (see
  sources.md S05).
- **Caption:** Maxwell and Halsted Streets, Chicago, April 1941 — the
  street-market corridor whose storefront vendors (including disc sellers
  like Ora Nelle) were the deep precedent for the Black record-store
  distribution ecology this brief documents.
- **Context in brief:** §6 Historical Context; the baseline for the
  distribution-node argument.
- **Publication check:** confirm the exact LOC item page via the reproduction
  number before going live.
