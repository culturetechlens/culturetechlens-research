## V9 — Personality photography (portraits of on-air talent)

- **What was sought:** Rights-cleared portraits of WVON-era personalities (Herb Kent, Pervis Spann, Lu Palmer, E. Rodney Jones, Yvonne Daniels, Daddy-O Daylie).
- **What was found:** No suitable images under this brief's rights policy. Illinois' deceased-personality publicity rights extend 50 years after death, so post-1976 deaths (Kent 2016, Palmer 2004, Spann 2022, Daniels 1991) remain restricted; no LOC/Smithsonian/Commons image with individually verified license and clear subject identity met the bar. Known candidates — the WVON "Good Guys" photo and Herb Kent publicity images — circulate widely online but with stripped or unknown provenance.
- **Decision:** Omit personalities rather than publish on uncertain rights. The five sourced visuals (signage, tower, studios, label building, neighborhood) carry the story without risk.
- **Status:** **unavailable-with-reason**.
