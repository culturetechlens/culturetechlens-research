## Priority source watch — Johnson Publishing Company Archive

**Status as of 2026-10-09:** NOT YET USABLE at file level. Recorded here as a permanent watch item for CTL's photo archive.

- **What it is:** The Johnson Publishing Company photo archive (Ebony/Jet, 1942–2019): 3M+ negatives/slides, 983,000 photographs, 166,000 contact sheets, 9,000 audio/video recordings.
- **Ownership chain:** JPC bankruptcy 2019 → purchased at auction (~$30M) by consortium (Ford Foundation, J. Paul Getty Trust, John D. and Catherine T. MacArthur Foundation, Andrew W. Mellon Foundation, Smithsonian Institution) → sole ownership transferred July 28, 2022 to the Getty Research Institute and the Smithsonian National Museum of African American History and Culture (NMAAHC). Physically housed at NMAAHC, Washington, DC.
- **Digitization:** Getty Trust committed $30M to processing and digitization; images released in curated batches (e.g., Getty's 2025 "$3 Bill: Evidence of Queer Lives" exhibition featured JPCA material; NMAAHC press selections). Individual photographs are NOT yet individually cataloged and published with item-level reuse licenses.
- **Why it matters for this brief:** Ebony and Jet were Chicago-published Black institutions covering exactly this brief's subjects (Defender, WVON, Black Chicago 1960s–2000s, South/West Side life). When item-level access opens, it is the single highest-value source for v2.0 of this brief's photography.
- **Action:** Re-check NMAAHC collection portal (https://nmaahc.si.edu/explore/collection) and Getty Research Institute JPCA project pages semi-annually. When individual JPC images publish with CC0/CC BY or other clear licenses, mint new CTL-IMG records continuing this series (next: CTL-IMG-2026-0011).
- **Provenance references:** NMAAHC transfer announcement (https://nmaahc.si.edu/about/news/ford-mellon-and-macarthur-foundations-transfer-sole-ownership-historic-ebony-and-jet); The Art Newspaper, 2022-08-01 (https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2022/08/01/ebony-jet-photo-archives-getty-research-institute-smithsonian-national-museum-african-american-history-culture); Getty JPCA feature (https://www.getty.edu/news/drag-balls-johnson-publishing-company-archive-photos/).
