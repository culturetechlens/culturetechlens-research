# Evidence File CTL-EF-EVENT-000001 — E2 Nightclub Tragedy, Chicago, 2003

**Companion to dossier:** CTL-RD-EVENT-000001 (E2 Nightclub Tragedy)
**Status:** Research · **Version:** 1.0 (2026-09-28)
**Sensitivity note:** This file records the evidence machinery behind claims about a mass-casualty event. Victims are treated collectively and with dignity; individual names appear only where families have spoken in reputable reporting and the name serves a documented public-record purpose.

## 1 · Research Question

**What can CTL responsibly establish about the E2 nightclub stampede of February 17, 2003 — what happened, who was responsible under the law, what regulatory changes followed, and what remains unknown or disputed?**

Sub-questions the claim ledger clusters around: (a) the documented sequence of the night; (b) the venue's licensing and regulatory history; (c) the emergency response; (d) criminal and civil legal outcomes; (e) regulatory changes caused by E2 versus merely contemporaneous ones; (f) memorialization and the event's place in Chicago nightlife-safety history.
