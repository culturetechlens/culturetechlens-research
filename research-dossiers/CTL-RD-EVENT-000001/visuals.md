## Data-driven graphics (recommended builds at publication)

### G1 — Timeline of the night (2003-02-17, CST)
Data: pepper spray ~2:18am (city account, Time Out 2012) → 911 calls 2:24am / 2:25am → fire department on scene 2:28am → 21 dead, 50+ injured. Mark the 2:18 time as city-attributed (SUPPORTED) and note the response-time dispute (CLAIM-038, DISPUTED). Horizontal timeline, black/deep red/gold CTL palette.

### G2 — Legal proceedings timeline (2002–2015)
Data: 2002-07 closure order → 2003-02-17 tragedy → 2007-01 manslaughter acquittals → 2008 charges dropped (Kyles) → 2009-11-25 contempt convictions → 2011-11-16 vacatur → 2013-04-04 Supreme Court reinstatement → 2015-11-20 resentencing (probation). All dates VERIFIED. Annotate the Wikipedia date conflation (CLAIM-037) as a caution.

### G3 — Casualty and capacity dataset (D1)
Bar/figure graphic from §25 Dataset D1: 21 deaths, 12W/9M split, 50+ injured, ~240 capacity vs. ~1,152–1,500 occupants. Source line on every figure.

### G4 — Regulatory aftermath map
Three enacted reforms (city ordinance 2003; IL state law 2003; Cousins-Zopp enforcement changes 2003–04) vs. one rejected proposal (promoter ordinance 2008–09). Caused-by-E2 vs. contemporaneous distinction made visual.

### G5 — Site map
2347 S. Michigan Ave. within Motor Row District, South Loop. District boundaries labeled with designation dates (Chicago Landmark 2000-12-13; NRHP 2002-11-18). Built from documented addresses only.
