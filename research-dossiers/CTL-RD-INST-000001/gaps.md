## 3. Priority archival targets 🟢

1. **Harsh Collection business records** (Defender institutional and business records, 1905–present per BPM-003 track-a) — the single highest-value target: subscription books, agent contracts, circulation audits, lease/deed dates for both buildings (refines the 1956–1960 retention question at CLM-026 and the 2005 exit at CLM-027).
2. **Pullman Company employment and route records** — porter identities, routes, and whether any carried papers officially or unofficially (CLM-008/CLM-040).
3. **Sengstacke Enterprises corporate and trust records** — the 1975 trust instrument, the 2002 sale file (CLM-025/CLM-033/CLM-034).
4. **1940s Defender issues (ProQuest)** — the "Victory Through Unity" edition; Double V coverage; the 1948 Truman-commission context.
5. **1915–1919 Defender issues (ProQuest)** — the *Birth of a Nation* campaign editorials and the 1919 riot coverage at the artifact level (CLM-014 is VERIFIED but issue-level documentation deepens it).

## 4. Future research questions

1. Who were the named subscription agents and porter carriers? (Oral histories with Pullman porter families may be the only surviving route.)
2. What did the Defender's Southern readership actually do with the paper — how did reception differ by city, church, and barbershop?
3. How did the 2002–2003 ownership change reshape editorial policy and staffing?
4. What is the Real Times Media leadership picture after 2019?
5. Which women staffers and stringers shaped the Defender's newsroom culture beyond the named stars (Payne, Brooks)?
6. What happened to the Michigan Avenue building's press plant after the ~2005 exit?
7. Can the 1929–2019 circulation gap be filled with any audited data points (post-ABC-resignation, the paper left ABC in late 2004)?

## 5. The largest gap

The fine mechanics of distribution — agent contracts, commission rates, headcount (CLM-039); porter payment, exact routes, quantities, named carriers (CLM-040). The dossier can prove the network existed and mattered (CLM-008, CLM-010); it cannot say how it worked in detail. This is the dossier's largest gap and its highest-priority research program.
