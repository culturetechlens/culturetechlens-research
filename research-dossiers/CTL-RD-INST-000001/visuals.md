## 3. Data graphics (CTL-produced; use where imagery is unavailable)

All graphics use the CTL palette (black / deep red / gold) and cite their dossier sections as data sources.

### GFX-001 — Distribution network map (preferred lead graphic)

- **Data:** CLM-008 / dossier §§04, 24. Rail corridors from Chicago southward via Pullman routes; drop nodes at barbershops and churches in Southern cities; the 1916–1917 letter origins (Jacksonville, FL; Lutcher, LA; Bessemer, AL; New Orleans; Memphis) as reception markers.
- **Caveats to print on the graphic:** "Precise routes, drop points, and quantities are reconstructed; only the corridor structure and the named letter origins are source-verified. Agent contracts and payment are unverified — see §15 CLM-039/040-equivalent records."
- **Note:** The map must not depict concealment or train-tossing (CLM-040, quarantined as legend).

### GFX-002 — Chicago headquarters timeline map

- **Data:** Dossier §07. 3435 S. Indiana Ave. (1920–1956, retained to 1960; landmark 1998); 2400/2410 S. Michigan Ave. (1956–2003, presses to 2005); 200 S. Michigan Ave., 17th floor (2005–2009); 4445 S. MLK Jr. Dr. (2009–present).
- **Format:** Small-multiples map or annotated timeline with map insets; gold markers on black base.

### GFX-003 — Sengstacke chain footprint map

- **Data:** Dossier §06 / CLM-021. Chicago Defender (1905), Michigan Chronicle/Detroit (1936), Tri-State Defender/Memphis (1951), New Pittsburgh Courier (relaunched 1967).
- **Format:** Four-node network map with founding/relaunch dates.

### GFX-004 — Circulation by era (chart)

- **Data:** CLM-012 (1929 peak ~250,000 copies), CLM-033 (2019: ~16,000 print / ~475,000 monthly web).
- **Format:** Step/discrete-era bars — never a smooth trend line (there are no data points between 1929 and 2019 to interpolate).
- **Caveat:** "2019 figures are publisher/media-reported (Nieman Lab), not audited."
