## 1. Research Question

What can CTL responsibly establish about WVON — Chicago's heritage Black radio station (1963–present) — from primary evidence: its founding, ownership, frequency, format, studio, personnel, civil-rights and political roles, and the myths that surround it?

## Per-claim records

### EF-C-001

**Claim:** WHFC began in 1926 in Chicago under owner Richard W. Hoffman.
**Evidence For:** Ghrist, *Valley Voices* (1996), pp.358–364: WHFC began in 1926 at the Hotel Flanders, owner Richard W. Hoffman; *Broadcasting–Telecasting* 1948-10-25 "AM Histories: Chicago" study lists WHFC.
**Evidence Against:** None found. (Searched: Ghrist 1996; Broadcasting 1948; FCC card references.)
**Primary + Secondary Sources:** Secondary: Ghrist 1996. Primary-adjacent: *Broadcasting–Telecasting* 1948-10-25.
**Source Evaluation:** Ghrist is a specialist radio-history reference, no commercial interest in the claim. Broadcasting 1948 is a contemporary trade account.
**Corroboration:** Two independent sources converge (Ghrist + 1948 trade press).
**Conflicts/Contradictions:** None found.
**Confidence Classification:** SUPPORTED — convergent secondary + contemporary trade account, but no direct FCC primary consulted in this pass.
**CTL Finding:** WHFC's 1926 origin under Hoffman is established by the best available references.
**What would change it:** FCC history cards for WHFC (pre-1963) would upgrade to VERIFIED; a contradictory FCC start date would force re-examination.
