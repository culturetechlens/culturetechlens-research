# CTL-RD-MEDIA-000001 — Gaps

## Structural gaps (archival)

1. **Chess-era economics.** No advertiser lists, revenue figures, or sales materials recovered. Only fragmentary traces: Billboard 1951 (national advertisers on Black shows — WHFC era), Billboard 1964 ratings. The station's commercial power is attested but not quantified. *Path:* Chicago Defender advertising columns 1963–69; Chess Records business papers (if archived); Arbitron/Pulse ratings books.

3. **Broadcast tapes.** 1960s–70s audio survives only as collector airchecks (rights-encumbered). *Path:* licensed institutional use; HistoryMakers oral histories as the cleared spoken-word record.
