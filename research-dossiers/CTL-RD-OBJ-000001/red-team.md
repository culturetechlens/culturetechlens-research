   attribution.

   Cheryl Morrow's testimony only; EF-005 keeps it SUPPORTED and gaps.md
   calls for sales records. Not upgraded to VERIFIED.

   No: the fist variant is the best-documented commercial/museum artifact,
   but C01–C09 cover the plain form fully; advertising and Morrow history
   are non-fist evidence.
4. *Could the Panther-commission story be true but suppressed?* —
   UNVERIFIABLE means exactly that; the dossier states the absence rather
   than asserting falsehood (EF-011).
