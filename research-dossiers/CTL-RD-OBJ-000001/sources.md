# Source Registry — CTL-RD-OBJ-000001: The Afro Pick

## Primary sources

- **U.S. Design Patent Des. 238,821** — "COMB HANDLE"; Anthony R. Romani
  (Hollidaysburg, PA); assignee Antonios Manufacturing Inc., Altoona, PA;
  granted 1976-02-10. Quoted verbatim in Makematic; retrievable via USPTO/
  Google Patents.
- **NMAAHC object record 2014.125.2** — "Afro hair comb with black fist
  design"; Eden Enterprise, Skokie IL; made in China; 2002–2014; "METAL
  FISTPIK"; gift of Elaine Nichols; "No Known Copyright Restrictions."
- **NMAAHC object record 2014.182.99** — "Wood hair comb from Ghana"; 1950s;
  gift of the William & Mattye Reed family; on view.
- **MoMA collection record 215.2019** — "Black Power Afro pick hair comb,"
  1972–1976, injection-molded plastic, Antonio's Manufacturing; image
  licensing via Art Resource/Scala.
- **Fitzwilliam Museum Annual Report 2012–13** — "Origins of the Afro Comb"
  exhibition record; plus quadrennial case study ("From Prisoners to
  Curators").
- **NYT, "MILITARY BARBERS LEARN TO CUT AFRO," Jan 31, 1971, p. 19** —
  cited via obituaries; full text not read in this research.

## Secondary sources (strong)

- Ashton, S.-A. "Radical Objects: The Black Fist Afro Comb," *History
  Workshop* (2014) — scholarly; design history, patent dates, British
  confiscations, Nigerian manufacture, plural meanings.
- Makematic, "Telling Stories of Artifacts – The Raised Fist Afro Comb" —
  quotes patent verbatim; corrects Bundles/Childrey fist attribution.
- ND Insider/USA Today, "Untangled from politics, the proud Afro hairstyle
  rises again" (2017) — family interviews (Romani Jr., A'Lelia Bundles).
- Risen, Clay. NYT obituary of Willie Lee Morrow (July 6, 2022).
- NPR/WCBE obituary (July 8, 2022) — Cheryl Morrow testimony; DoD program.
- Ebony — Morrow obituary; George E. Johnson profile (Johnson Products).
- Andscape — Joan Johnson/Soul Train; Black-pride marketing strategy.
- NMAAHC essays: "Strands of Inspiration"; "Sizzle" (hot-comb history).
- York University journal — "Resistance in My Strands and a Pick in My
  Hands" (1972 Duke & Raveen ad).
- Retro Musings — "Duke & Raveen ~ Hair Care Adverts [1964–1972]" (ad
  archive; rights unverified).
- UCL Culture Blog — Pia Edqvist, conservator (Oct 28, 2013).
- Fresh Air Archive — A'Lelia Bundles interview; Living on Earth — hot-comb
  myth.
