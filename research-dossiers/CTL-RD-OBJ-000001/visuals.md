## Anti-speculation rule on visuals

The Smithsonian's fist-pick image documents the *artifact*; NMAAHC's
classification documents its *interpretation*. Do not pair the image with an
unsupported claim (e.g., a Panther-commission caption). Captions must carry
the claim grade: VERIFIED for the artifact and its museum classification;
UNRESOLVED for the Panther-commission story — so it is not captioned at all.
