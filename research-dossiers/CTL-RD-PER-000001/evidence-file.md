### CLAIM-001

**Claim:** Ida Bell Wells was born July 16, 1862, in Holly Springs, Mississippi.

**Evidence For ↔ Evidence Against:**
- For: Tennessee Encyclopedia gives "16 July 1862–25 Mar. 1931 ... born in Holly Springs, Mississippi"; Rutgers CAWP, Facing History, myblackhistory.net, HBCU Buzz, and her autobiography's account converge on July 16, 1862 / Holly Springs.
- Against: none found. (Searched: variant birth dates; none surfaced in this pass.)

**Primary + Secondary Sources:**
- Primary: Wells, *Crusade for Justice* (autobiography) — birth/family account.
- Secondary: Tennessee Encyclopedia (scholarly); Rutgers CAWP; Facing History & Ourselves; UXL Biographies.

**Source Evaluation:** Tennessee Encyclopedia is a scholarly state reference with editorial review — strong secondary. Autobiography is primary but self-reported on birth date (low incentive to misstate; convergent with all secondaries). No circularity detected: the encyclopedia and CAWP draw on distinct scholarly bases.

**Corroboration:** Four independent secondary sources plus the primary account converge; independent, not repetitions of one another.

**Conflicts/Contradictions:** None on date or place.

**Confidence Classification:** VERIFIED — multiple independent sources including a strong secondary; no counterevidence.

**CTL Finding:** Wells was born July 16, 1862, in Holly Springs, Mississippi.

**What would change it:** A primary vital record (birth register, family Bible) contradicting the date; none is known to survive from an enslaved birth in 1862 Mississippi.
