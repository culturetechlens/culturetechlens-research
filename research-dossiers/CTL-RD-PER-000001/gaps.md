**Version:** 1.0.0 — 2026-09-28
**Expands:** dossier §28

> **CULTURETECHLENS** — *Culture, Clearly Seen.* — Black Cultural Intelligence

## Largest gap

The surviving *Memphis Free Speech and Headlight* corpus and business records
(1889–1892): issues, mastheads, ownership mechanics (shares, purchase price,
partners beyond J. L. Fleming), and the office's verified street address. This
is the primary-evidence ceiling on the dossier's central "woman publisher"
thesis — the press that made her investigation distributable, and that was
itself destroyed as a target.
