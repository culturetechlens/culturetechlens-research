## Circular-sourcing check

No VERIFIED claim rests on a single source repeated across outlets. The strongest
VERIFIED items pair a primary account (autobiography) with a contemporary or
independent source (NY Sun/AP dispatch; four institutional secondaries for the
1913 procession; convergent scholarly references for birth/enslavement).
Popular-source convergence (e.g., birth date) was checked for independence:
Tennessee Encyclopedia and Rutgers CAWP draw on distinct scholarly bases.
