## EF-001 — Community-area identity (C-001)

1. **Research Question:** Is Washington Park a formally recognized Chicago community area, and what is its number?
2. **Claim:** Washington Park is Chicago community area #40, on the South Side.
3. **Evidence For ↔ Evidence Against:** The City of Chicago's official community-area system lists 77 areas; Washington Park appears as #40 in every official listing (City planning documents, CMAP snapshots, Burnham Plan centennial appendix). No source disputes this.
4. **Primary + Secondary Sources:** Primary: City of Chicago community-area maps and CMAP Community Data Snapshot (Washington Park). Secondary: Wikipedia community-area article (cites City/CMAP); Burnham Plan Centennial appendix (areas 1–77).
5. **Source Evaluation:** Authoritative (City/CMAP). Secondary sources consistent.
6. **Corroboration:** Wikipedia infobox, CMAP snapshot, and OSM polygon all agree on #40 / South Side.
7. **Conflicts/Contradictions:** None found.
8. **Confidence Classification:** VERIFIED.
9. **CTL Finding:** Washington Park is Community Area 40, South Side Chicago.
10. **What would change it:** A City redistricting — none has occurred since the 1920s.
