# Visuals — CTL-RD-PLACE-000001

**Branding:** All public-facing CTL visuals carry the CULTURETECHLENS wordmark, "Culture, Clearly Seen.", "Black Cultural Intelligence," and the black / deep red / gold identity.
**Rule:** No AI-generated historical imagery or people. No scraped or uncleared third-party images.

## Produced originals (data-driven, in `visuals/`)

1. **`visuals/population-timeline.png`** — Census population 1930–2020 (data: U.S. Census via Rob Paral). Shows 1950 peak (56,856), 2010 trough (11,717), 2020 rebound (12,707). Build script: `visuals/build_visuals.py`.
2. **`visuals/boundary-schematic.png`** — Schematic (explicitly NOT to scale) of CA 40 with the 372-acre park overlay; marks the 60th St. south line, the Washington Park Subdivision (Woodlawn) and Washington Park Court District (Grand Boulevard) as outside-but-adjacent. Vintage stated: OSM + CMAP snapshot, 2026.
3. **`visuals/relationship-diagram.png`** — Key entity relationships: gold edges = VERIFIED, dashed gray = SUPPORTED; adjacency corrections in deep red.

## Required before any publication

- **Historic photograph:** John H. White, "Picnic in Washington Park, 1973." Verify creator, catalog identifier, repository (likely National Archives/Documerica), exact date, and public-domain status. DO NOT publish until cleared (see gaps.md G-07).
- **Authoritative geographic map:** Replace the schematic with a true-projection map from the City of Chicago or CMAP community-area polygon dataset, with dataset vintage stated, before any public release.
