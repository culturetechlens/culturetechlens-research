# CTL-DR-BPM-003 — Sources
## Merged Bibliography (all five tracks, deduplicated)

**Legend:** P = primary (digitized issue, business/legislative record, archival finding aid, oral history, contemporaneous document); I = institutional/university collection or reference; J = reputable journalism; S = scholarly (peer-reviewed/book/thesis); G = general reference (Wikipedia, newspapers.com-era aggregators — finding aid only, provisional at best). Access notes where known.
