# Gaps — CTL-DR-BVM-005: Bronzeville: Black Metropolis

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**How to read:** Grouped by priority. Each gap states what is missing, why it matters for the flagship artifact, and the concrete primary-source retrieval that would close it.

---

## P0 — Close before any flagship artifact ships

1. **Venue-by-venue primary verification (Sanborn maps, city directories, Defender archival issues).**
   No primary Sanborn maps, city directories, or Defender archival issues were consulted for the jazz-venue layer. Lindstrom's compilation is load-bearing and single-sourced for several venues. Retrieval: Sanborn fire-insurance maps (Chicago, 1920s-50s editions) for the Stroll and 47th/South Parkway blocks; Polk/R.L. city directories for venue addresses and proprietors; Defender entertainment pages, 1918-1932.
2. **Direct Census/NHGIS verification of the population series.**
   The Douglas + Grand Boulevard combined series (1950-2020) derives from Rob Paral's compilation via Wikipedia. Retrieval: NHGIS tract/community-area tables, 1930-2020, for Douglas and Grand Boulevard separately; pre-1930 ward-level where feasible.
3. **Copyright and usage-rights review for archival images.**
   No rights review has been done. Before any visual artifact: identify target images (Regal/Savoy marquees, Defender front pages, Binga/Overton portraits), determine rights holders, and clear or substitute. This is a gating item.
4. **Current (late-2026) status checks:** Bronzeville Lakefront Phase 1 completion; Obama Presidential Center opening status; National Museum of Gospel Music construction/funding at Pilgrim Baptist; Sunset Café building occupant/mural status after 2017; Unity Hall current condition; Chicago Defender Building address/current use.
   Several of these were last confirmed 2021-2025. An artifact that says "now" must re-verify.

## P1 — Close to reach primary-source authority

5. **Lake Meadows and aggregate urban-renewal displacement counts.**
   The repeated "18,000 displaced" figure is unverified; exact Lake Meadows displacement is unresolved. Retrieval: CHA project files; Illinois Institute of Technology / Mercy Hospital project records; contemporary Defender and Tribune coverage, 1946-1955.
6. **Dan Ryan Expressway displacement and business-loss counts.**
   Clearance 1957-58 is Verified; counts (10,000? 12,000?) are Unresolved. Retrieval: Illinois Division of Highways right-of-way acquisition files; Cook County recorder data; Defender coverage of corridor businesses, 1957-62.
7. **Defender archival issues (1919-1960s).**
   Consulted only via secondary citation. The Defender is simultaneously the subject and the archive: its pages are the primary source for the naming-origin story, venue advertising, Bud Billiken, and the "Great Northern Drive."
8. **Oral-history consultation.**
   Identified but not consulted: CHM *Neighborhoods: Keepers of Culture* (1992) Bronzeville portion; Studs Terkel Radio Archive; The HistoryMakers interviews; Sherry Williams/Bronzeville Historical Society video collection (incl. Washington Park races and 1973 Regal demolition footage); Timuel Black's *Bridges of Memory* and his 36 DuSable/Phillips interviews (1996; repository unresolved). Retrieval: listen/view, then encode claims into the evidence model.

## P2 — Structural enrichments

9. **Regal Theater architect.**
   Unresolved; no attribution should be published without building-permit or trade-press verification. Retrieval: Chicago building permits (1927-28); *American Architect* / *Architectural Forum* coverage of the National Theater Corp. project.
10. **Savoy Ballroom closing year (1948 vs. 1954).**
    Preserved as alternatives. Retrieval: Defender and Tribune entertainment listings, 1946-1956; city business-license records.
11. **Dreamland Café address (3520 vs. 3618 S. State) and Apex Club address (330 E. 35th — corroboration).**
    Retrieval: city directories 1914-1928; Sanborn maps of the block.
12. **Sunset vs. Grand Terrace conflation.**
    Online sources routinely merge them. Retrieval: venue licenses and Defender ads, 1921-1937.
13. **Working-class institutional layer.**
    Named barbershops, beauty shops, storefront churches, and Bronzeville-specific fraternal lodges remain thin. Retrieval: Defender classifieds and display ads; church anniversary programs; Illinois corporation/charter records.
14. **Douglass National Bank founding year (1921 vs. 1922 vs. 1923).**
    Relationship verified; year disputed. Retrieval: Office of the Comptroller of the Currency charter records; bank's own publications.
15. **Robert Taylor Homes unit count (4,321 vs. 4,415).**
    Retrieval: CHA development records.
16. **Hansberry v. Lee decision day (Nov 12 vs. Nov 13 vs. Dec 12, 1940).**
    Retrieval: U.S. Reports, 311 U.S. 32 — the printed volume settles this in one lookup.
17. **Business institutions not researched:** Bronzeville Chamber of Commerce; Bronzeville Alliance. Retrieval: organizational records, Defender business pages.
18. **Current parcel uses for lost venues and landmark buildings.**
    Several parcels (Palm Tavern building, Chicago Bee Building upper floors, Overton Hygienic occupancy) lack current-condition confirmation. Retrieval: site survey / Cook County parcel data.

## P3 — Nice to have

19. **Second-ghetto quantitative framing.**
    The Hirsch/Venkatesh/Wilson literature is represented but not worked into a Bronzeville-specific quantitative argument (e.g., manufacturing-job loss by tract, 1950-1980). Retrieval: NHGIS + County Business Patterns.
20. **Comparative district framing.**
    How Bronzeville's preservation outcomes compare to peer Black commercial districts (Harlem's 125th, Atlanta's Auburn Avenue, Durham's Black Wall Street). Useful for the artifact's "what's distinctive" layer.
21. **Full provenance pass on famous-resident claims.**
    Residency verification is enforced as policy; a systematic pass over every named person in the entity set would close remaining Provisional residencies (Nat King Cole, Earl Hines, etc.).
