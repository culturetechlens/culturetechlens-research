# Sources — CTL-DR-BVM-005: Bronzeville: Black Metropolis

**Date compiled:** 2026-09-28
**Method:** Consolidated and deduplicated from the six research tracks (A–F). Unique-source estimate: **~140 distinct sources** — roughly **35 primary** and **~105 secondary**. Wikipedia entries are counted as tertiary and are not listed individually here; see Note 2.

## Note on the house evidence standard
- Every major claim in `entities.json` and `timeline.json` carries: claim, evidence, source, primary/secondary classification, publication/date, uncertainty, and contradictory evidence where it exists, and is graded **Verified / Provisional / Disputed / Unresolved**.
- `relationships.json` contains **Verified relationships only**. Nothing is manufactured.
- Grade normalization: Track A's **CORROBORATED** maps to **Verified** where two or more independent credible secondary sources agree and no material contradiction exists.

## Note 1 — Primary sources are under-represented
This package was built from the secondary literature and public records summaries. The high-priority primary sources still to be retrieved in person are listed in `gaps.md`. A flagship CTL artifact must not claim primary-source authority where it has only secondary synthesis.

## Note 3 — The Lindstrom compilation
Bo Lindstrom's 2012 venue-by-venue chronology is this package's richest single source for jazz-era venues. Where a Lindstrom-only claim is uncorroborated it is graded **Provisional**, and internal Lindstrom inconsistencies (e.g., Grand Terrace's 1929/1930 closing) are preserved as Disputed rather than resolved.
