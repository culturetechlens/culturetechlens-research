## Scholarly monographs and peer-reviewed articles

1. **Robert E. Weems Jr., _Black Business in the Black Metropolis: The Chicago Metropolitan Assurance Company, 1925–1985_** (Indiana University Press, 1996). The foundational scholarly history of Chicago Metropolitan, based on company records and oral interviews. Used via thefreelibrary.com review/summary and the Library of Congress research-guide summary.
   - https://www.thefreelibrary.com/Black+Business+in+the+Black+Metropolis-a059025065
2. **Robert C. Puth, "Supreme Life: The History of a Negro Life Insurance Company, 1919–1962," _Business History Review_** (Cambridge). Peer-reviewed; the first scholarly study of an individual Negro life insurance firm.
   - https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/business-history-review/article/supreme-life-the-history-of-a-negro-life-insurance-company-19191962/ (landing page; accessed via BVM-005)
3. **Robert E. Weems Jr., _The Merchant Prince of Black Chicago: Anthony Overton and the Building of a Financial Empire_** (University of Illinois Press, 2020). Scholarly biography of Overton (Victory Life, Douglass National Bank, Overton Hygienic Building).
4. **Todd M. Michney, "Black Life Insurance Companies, Mortgages, and African American Homeownership Before 1964"** (Yale economics working paper, 2022; Cambridge journal version 2025). Documents Black insurers' mortgage lending, including Chicago Metropolitan's $6,000 → $281,000 mortgage growth (1938–1945).
   - https://economics.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/Michney_Black Insurance Cos Mortgages AfAm Hwp.pdf
   - https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/5DEFB239B5BE0530F31A1AFDB3FDD87A/S0007680525101360a.pdf/black_life_insurance_companies_mortgages_and_african_american_homeownership_before_1964.pdf
5. **Robert Weems, OHCHR submission on African American-owned insurance companies.** Documents the 1962–1992 decline (50 → 23 firms) and the 1963 NIA convention's failed integration push.
6. **David Abner III, "Some Aspects of the Growth of Negro Legal Reserve Life Insurance Companies, 1930–1960"** (Indiana University doctoral thesis, 1962). Source of the 1960 Supreme Life third-largest ranking and NIA aggregate figures (via blackhistoryheroes.com; secondhand — verify before publication).
7. **Nordin, "The National Negro Bankers Association"** (academic article via BVM-005). Documents Douglass National Bank as "the only surviving black bank in Chicago following the closing of the Binga Bank" and its 1932 failure.

## Federal and public-institution sources

8. **Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City, "Let Us Put Our Money Together" (2019).** Documents Binga (first Black-owned bank in the Northern U.S., 1908), Douglass (1922), and First National Bank of Boley, OK (first nationally chartered Black bank, 1921).
9. **Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, _Economic Perspectives_ (2017), Toussaint-Comeau & Newberger.** Documents Seaway's January 2017 shutdown and transfer to State Bank of Texas → Self-Help Federal Credit Union.
   - https://www.chicagofed.org/~/media/publications/economic-perspectives/2017/ep2017-4-pdf.pdf

    - https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64000173_text
11. **Library of Congress, African Americans in Business research guide.** Summarizes Weems's work on Chicago Metropolitan and Overton; key secondary synthesis.
    - https://guides.loc.gov/african-americans-in-business/businesses-industries/banking-insurance-finance
12. **Congressional Record, 113th Congress (2013).** Tributes to Seaway chairman Jacoby Dickens (Rep. Danny K. Davis; H.Res. 191).
    - https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-159/issue-69/house-section/article/H2663-1

14. **ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer.** IRS 990 data confirming IBPOEW lodges' 501(c)(8) fraternal-beneficiary-society status (218 Booker T. Washington Lodge).
