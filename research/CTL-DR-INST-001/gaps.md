# CTL-DR-INST-001 — Gaps, Unknowns, and Next Investigations

## What we know
- Function, governance, funding, dependents, replacement, survival, and records location for 81 institutions (329 graded claims: 174 Verified).
- 60 verified dependency edges; 159 dated events.
- Survival Index: 87 rows (descriptive indicators only); decade matrix 260/260 cells.
- Governance-transition-predicts-survival pattern; institutional-vs-functional survival distinction.

## What we think we know (Provisional — verify before public use)
- ~115 Provisional claims, including most decade-matrix "weak/moderate" cells.
- 50 Provisional Survival Index rows (need date-level verification).
- The "no Black-American-owned bank headquartered in Chicago" inference — recheck against current FDIC MDI list.
- GN Bank's 2025 OCC clearance (Ghanaian diaspora press quoting an OCC letter; no U.S. confirmation).
- Self-reported figures: CCPA membership/scholarships, SSCFCU lending, Cosmopolitan Chamber impact.

## What remains unknown
1. **Fraternal lodge minutes** — Odd Fellows, Knights of Pythias (highest-value missing records). Candidate repositories: Harsh Collection, CHM, UIC, NPS/NRHP files.
2. **Records locations** for most entities — only a minority have verified archival homes.
3. **Founder rosters** — Highland Bank, Cosmopolitan Chamber, ISF's 11 of 13 founders; dues/premium schedules and audited finances broadly.
4. **Closure dates** — Negro Fellowship League, Frederick Douglass Center, Appomattox Club, West Side Organization, American Giants' later years.
5. **UNIA / Chicago Garvey movement** — the most obvious missing mass-membership institution.
6. **Social clubs** — unknown in all 13 decades of the matrix.
7. **Professional associations** beyond physicians/lawyers — dentists, nurses, pharmacists, accountants, realtists, businesswomen's associations.
8. **Black boxing gyms / youth leagues** beyond Jackie Robinson West; sports beyond baseball.
9. **Post-1999 Black insurance** after Supreme Life; early Black radio pre-WVON; current fraternal-lodge activity.
10. **Supreme Life's 1999 takeover** — needs Illinois Dept. of Insurance primary source.

## Disputed items (do not present single versions)
- Emmett Till viewing crowd: 50,000–100,000 (do not cite one number).
- Amanda Smith Orphanage closure/fire: 1917 vs 1918.
- St. Monica founding: 1893 vs 1894; Pekin Theatre opening: 1904 vs 1905; Ethiopian Art Theatre: 1922/1923; Negro Chamber founding: 1931 vs 1933 (lineage Verified).
- WKKC first air date: 1975 vs 1979 (FCC records needed); WBGX ownership (Disputed).
- Chicago American Giants end date: 1952 / 1955-56 / 1958 revival.
- Urban Prep 2023 charter dispute — genuinely contested; needs CPS Board records and court filings.
