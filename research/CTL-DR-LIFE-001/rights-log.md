## General guidance for this workstream

- **Safest visual assets:** FSA-OWI Bronzeville photos (public domain, LOC) and Congressional Record excerpts.
- **Highest-risk:** Ebony/Jet covers, Defender page images, Jubilee Showcase footage, Terkel audio reuse.
- **Privacy note (per CTL-PRESERVE-001 constraint):** Family photograph albums and private-collection locations must never be exposed in public outputs; the Willis "sacred archive" framing is cited as scholarship, not as an invitation to publish private images.
