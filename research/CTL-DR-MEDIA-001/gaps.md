# CTL-DR-MEDIA-001 — Gaps Register
Date: 2026-09-28

## What we know
- The national arc of Black media ownership: print networks (NNPA 1940–present), the radio policy hinge (1978 tax certificate → 1996 Telecom Act), cable television (BET 1980 → Viacom 2000/2001), digital churn and platform dependence (2008–present).
- The current ownership map: three Black-owned national operators (Urban One, Allen Media Group, Essence Ventures) vs. non-Black-owned Black-targeted giants (BET/Paramount Skydance, iHeartMedia, Audacy).
- The four survival models (buy-backs, scale-and-diversification, nonprofit conversion, corporate-IP consolidation) and the family-ownership pattern in print.
- The archival landscape's major nodes (Schomburg, Howard Black Press Archives, LOC/Chronicling America, JPC Archive rescue) and documented cases of known loss.

## What we think we know (Provisional — needs corroboration)
- Ad-spend bypass figures (industry-reported, not independently verified).
- The causal link between platform algorithm changes and specific publishers' traffic declines (correlational).
- Founding/firstness claims in early radio (multiple Disputed).
- NNPA's current membership count and internal state (self-reported; leadership-crisis claims one-sided).
- 17 relationships held in `_tracks/held-relationships.json` pending a second source.

## What remains unknown
- The true scale of archival loss (survivorship bias is structural; Tennessee papers with zero extant copies imply unmeasured total loss).
- Current FCC Form 323-based Black-owned station counts (not reviewed in this pass).
- Streaming-carriage economics (deal terms undisclosed).
- Newsstand-specific Black-press circulation data (historical figures are self-reported/unaudited).
- Apple App Tracking Transparency's specific impact on Black publishers (Unresolved — no verified source captured).
- Whether AURN still counts as Black-owned (Access.1/Guggenheim control question — Disputed).
