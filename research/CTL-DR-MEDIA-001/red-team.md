# CTL-DR-MEDIA-001 — Red-Team Audit
Date: 2026-09-28 | Auditor: research coordinator (merge-time audit)

## Checklist results

1. **Unsupported claims — CONCERN, action taken.** 131 of 335 originally-Verified claims (39%) cited Wikipedia. Rule applied: claims whose only substantive source is Wikipedia (or Wikipedia + Alchetron, a Wikipedia mirror — not independent corroboration) were downgraded Verified → Provisional with an audit note appended to the uncertainty field. **38 claims downgraded** (full log in `_tracks/regrade-log.txt`). The remaining 93 Wikipedia-citing claims had independent corroborating sources and kept their grades. Re-sourcing the 38 is the #1 backlog item.
2. **Missing communities — FAIL.** Zero entities cover the Black LGBTQ+ press (e.g., BLK, Blacklight, or regional titles). No LGBTQ+ owners, publishers, or journalists appear as entities. Queued in gaps.md.
3. **Geographic bias — CONCERN.** Print covers 10+ cities (good spread: Pittsburgh, Baltimore, New York, Atlanta, Los Angeles, Philadelphia, Washington, Detroit, St. Louis, Houston). Radio skews South/Memphis/Atlanta plus New York/Philadelphia/Houston; the West Coast beyond the LA Sentinel is thin; the Midwest beyond Chicago-print is thin. TV/digital are national. No Pacific Northwest or Mountain West coverage.
4. **Famous-person bias — CONCERN.** 29 person entities skew to founders/owners/CEOs (Johnson, Hughes, Allen, Graves, Winfrey). Missing: pressroom workers, carriers, station engineers, rank-and-file journalists, ad salespeople — the labor layer of media infrastructure.
5. **Institutional bias — CONCERN.** Flagship institutions dominate (BET, Urban One, NNPA, AFRO). Small-market papers and stations are undercovered; post-1996 small-station casualties are under-documented (radio track flagged this explicitly).
6. **Survivorship bias — FAIL (structural).** Dead outlets vanish from the record by definition. The archives track documents the extreme case: Tennessee Black papers with no extant copies at all. No verifiable case of a specific Black station's tape archive being destroyed was found — an evidence gap, not evidence of absence. Any "decline" narrative built from survivors understates loss.
7. **Missing women — CONCERN.** 9 of 29 person entities are women (31% — better than BCI-001's 12.5%, still short). Documented: Cathy Hughes, Debra L. Lee, Oprah Winfrey, Morgan DeBaun, Angelica Nwandu, Sherrell Dorsey, Lauren Williams, Hazel B. Garland, Karen Carter Richards. **Missing as entities:** Charlotta Bass (California Eagle — major omission), Ida B. Wells (anti-lynching editor), Marvel Cooke, Alice Dunnigan, Martha Murphy. Hazel B. Garland has zero Verified claims — needs a dedicated pass.
8. **Missing working-class/everyday history — FAIL.** See #4. No carriers, press operators, or station staff.
9. **Weak sources — CONCERN, partially actioned.** Beyond the Wikipedia regrade: digital-track funding/traffic figures are founder-reported (primary but self-interested — labeled as such); NNPA internal-crisis claims (late 2025) are one-sided and unanswered (graded Disputed); company reach claims ("80% of Black America," "315M subscribers") are marketing figures (flagged as such in notes).
10. **Duplicate entities — PASS.** 22 cross-track merge groups resolved 34 duplicates into 140 canonical entities (`MED-001`–`MED-140`). Zero claim conflicts on merge (no identical claim carried different grades across tracks). Within-track duplicates: none found.
11. **Disputed dates — CONCERN, documented.** 18 Disputed + 3 Unresolved claims inventoried, including: BET sale price ($2.3B vs ~$3B) and closing year (2000 vs 2001); BET full-time launch (1982 vs 1983); ANP cessation (1964 vs 1967); Atlanta Daily World daily date (1931 vs 1932); Muhammad Speaks first year (1960 vs 1961); AURN merger year (1991 vs 1995); KPRS founding (1949/1950/1952); WURD purchase (2001 vs 2003); NNPA membership ("200+" vs 160 listed). Each is graded Disputed with competing claims recorded — do not resolve by picking one.
12. **Misleading causality — CONCERN, action taken.** comScore traffic declines (The Root −36%, EURweb −63%) are correlational with Google updates, NOT proven caused — labeled as such. The Defender-as-migration-catalyst style causal claims were not made here; where influence is asserted without evidence it is graded Provisional or excluded.
13. **Rights/copyright — FLAG FOR PAGE BUILD.** Magazine covers, broadcast clips, photographs (JPC Archive images are Getty/NMAAHC-managed), and song clips all require rights clearance. No media files are included in this package; the page build must treat every image/clip as rights-restricted until cleared.
14. **Sensitivity — PASS.** NNPA leadership-crisis material is handled as Disputed (one-sided sourcing noted); Group Black litigation claims are marked disputed; no doctrinal or personal judgments; public institutional history only.

## Residual risks for the page build
- Any "Verified" claim still resting on a single secondary source should be spot-checked before public display of the strongest assertions (deal values, audience figures).
- The ownership-vs-targeting distinction must be visually explicit on the page, or readers will misread Black-targeted assets as Black-owned.
- Disputed items should be shown as disputed (the "contested dates" interactive pattern from SCI-004 applies here).
