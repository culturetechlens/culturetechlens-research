# CTL-DR-PLACE-001 — Missing-Evidence Register & Future-Research Agenda
**63rd Street: A Cultural Biography of a Chicago Corridor · 2026-09-28**

## Missing-evidence register (prioritized)

### Tier 1 — would change grades from Provisional to Verified
1. **Chicago Defender archives, 1915–1975** (advertisements, mentions, social pages). Single richest untapped source. Would verify: theater/club dates, Black business ownership, everyday commerce, entertainment lineups.
2. **Sanborn fire insurance maps, 1925 & 1950** — theater parcels, 63rd & Halsted commercial core, building footprints and dates.
3. **Chicago building permits** — Tivoli, Tower, Southtown, Stratford, Englewood, WPNB, Sears, St. Rita, Shrine: construction dates and architects.
4. **chicago-l.org / CTA primary records** — station opening/closing dates (currently Wikipedia-only across the board).
5. **Cook County Recorder / tax / assessment records** — ownership chains; Sears exact corner; WPNB ownership history.

### Tier 2 — would fill named gaps
6. **Archdiocese of Chicago parish records** — St. Rita address and founding documentation (weakest link in the package).
7. **Denominational records + church anniversary books** — small-congregation founding dates; church mothers' and missionary societies' histories (addresses the missing-women gap).
8. **Chicago Public Schools archives** — school openings/closings on the corridor.
9. **City directories, 1920–1980** — Black professional services (banks, insurers, doctors, dentists, lawyers, real-estate offices); everyday shop addresses (Elman's, Regal Men's Shop, Rexall, Lauer Sisters).
10. **Chicago Housing Authority records** — Parkway Gardens / Ida B. Wells adjacency effects on the corridor.
11. **Englewood Union Station demolition permit / ICC valuation maps** — resolves late-1970s vs. 1982 dispute.
12. **Chicago History Museum photo collections** — then/now pairs; rights clearance required.

### Tier 3 — field and community work
13. **2026 site checks** — WPNB construction status, Englewood Square tenancy, Racine station condition, Shrine restoration progress, C&WI remnants (551 W. 63rd).
14. **Oral histories: beauty/barbershop culture** — the experience layer's largest void.
15. **Oral histories: congregational life** — the experience layer's second void.
16. **Mosque mapping** — no mosque on 63rd proper verified; gap, not absence.
17. **Chicago Lawn commercial survey** — document the vitality the decline narrative must not swallow.
18. **63rd Street platting date** — township survey / Cook County highway records.

## Uncertainty register (top items carried forward)

- Sears: 1929 vs. 1934; exact address unresolved.
- Englewood Mall: 1969 vs. ~1973 chronology.
- "Second-busiest shopping district": metric and primary source untraced.
- Halsted station opening date; pedestrian-mall end date.
- Tivoli: 6323 vs. 6325 S. Cottage Grove; demolition date.
- Maryland Theatre: 1928 vs. 1918. Daley's current address. Wieboldt's closure year.
- Arson/fire counts: no legal findings behind the claims.
- ACOG campus milestones (1977/1992): single secondary source.
- July 29, 1966 Halverson Realty march: single secondary source.

## Future-research agenda (sequenced)

**Phase 2A — archival pulls (highest grade-lift per effort):** Defender index → Sanborn/permit pull for ~25 priority properties → chicago-l.org verification sweep → Archdiocese + denominational records → city-directory sweep for professional services.

**Phase 2B — field program:** 2026 site checks with photography (rights-cleared); Chicago Lawn commercial survey; mosque mapping.

**Phase 2C — community memory:** oral-history capture for beauty/barbershop culture and congregational life; Defender social-page mining for women's organizational life (directly targets the red-team's largest blind spot).

**Phase 2D — visual layer (per master prompt):** then/now photography pairs; interactive street-by-year specification; building cards for the 25 verified properties; transit-map overlays; demographic-map series — gated on rights clearance (see red-team §13).

**Phase 2E — publication readiness:** direct-view verification of all secondhand newspaper citations by a browser-capable agent; Census API re-verification of the 1930–2020 tables; legal review of the 1968-flight/arson passages before public narrative use.
