## E. NOT consulted — the critical gap list

These are the highest-value unconsulted sources, in priority order:

1. **Chicago Defender archives, 1915–1975** — advertisements, mentions, social pages. The single richest Black-press source set for this corridor. Untouched by all five workers.
2. **Sanborn fire insurance maps (1925, 1950)** — theater parcels, the 63rd & Halsted commercial core, building footprints/dates. Highest-value cartographic record outstanding.
3. **Chicago building permits** — Tivoli, Tower, Southtown, Stratford, Englewood, WPNB, Sears, St. Rita, Shrine construction dates and architects.
4. **chicago-l.org / CTA primary records** — station opening/closing dates are overwhelmingly Wikipedia-only; this is the biggest single corroboration gap.
5. **Cook County Recorder / tax / assessment records** — ownership chains, exact Sears corner, WPNB ownership.
6. **Archdiocese of Chicago parish records** — St. Rita address and founding documentation.
7. **Denominational records and church anniversary books** — small-congregation founding dates (Greater St. John AME, St. John EMBC, St. Mary's M.B.).
8. **Chicago Public Schools archives** — school openings/closings on the corridor.
9. **Chicago Housing Authority records** — Parkway Gardens / Ida B. Wells adjacency effects.
10. **Chicago History Museum photo collections** — noted as leads per property; not searched.
11. **Englewood Union Station demolition permit / ICC valuation maps** — would resolve the late-1970s vs. 1982 dispute.
12. **2026 field verification** — WPNB construction status, Englewood Square tenancy, Racine station, Shrine restoration: all need fresh site checks.

## F. Source-quality summary

- Strongest sourcing: transit chronology (CTA/Wikipedia + Tribune citations), census demographics, HOLC/Mapping Inequality, jazz clubs (Defender-via-UChicago-guide), Tivoli (landmark report + trade press), 1966 marches (movement archive + press).
- Weakest sourcing: station-level dates (Wikipedia-only), Sears (YouTube), St. Rita address (TikTok), "second-busiest" origin (untraced), arson claims (no legal findings), Englewood Mall chronology.
- Rule applied throughout: no claim was upgraded past its evidence. Provisional stays Provisional until a primary source confirms it.
