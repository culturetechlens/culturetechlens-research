# Sources — CTL-DR-PRESERVE-001 · The Black Memory Emergency

> **CULTURETECHLENS** · *Culture, Clearly Seen.* · **Black Cultural Intelligence**

Consolidated, deduplicated bibliography across all six research tracks.
Each entry is grouped by the track where it was first gathered; entries cited by multiple tracks are annotated.
Labels: **Primary** = produced by the institution/party directly involved (institutional pages, filings, company announcements, staff blogs, recordings, published finding aids). **Secondary** = press, journalism, scholarship, commentary. **Tertiary** = encyclopedia/reference summaries (used only where corroborated by stronger sources).
All web sources accessed **2026-09-27 to 2026-09-28**, unless a publication date is given in the entry.

Note: the **framework** track has no separate source list; its eight worked examples cite evidence gathered in the other five tracks, credited within each example.
