## Zone 1 — Industrial Chicago's Black technical workforce (the largest gap)

The package documents Black operators at Pullman (porters, 38 A/C systems, 43 berth types — Verified), Gary Works cranemen, and NACA human computers — but **employer-level archival evidence for Black engineers, technicians, and skilled workers in industrial Chicago itself remains thin**. Specifically missing:

- Black machinists in Chicago WWII defense plants (only a 1930 census-era claim found; insufficient).
- Black workers at the Pullman Works car-building plant (Chicago) — known context, no verified operator-technology claims collected.
- Black telephone operators on Chicago switchboards (Illinois Bell) — no verified case.
- Black CTA "L"/streetcar motormen and conductors — no verified firsts; only Mary Wallace (bus, 1974) documented.
- Black telegraph operators (likely significant on railroads) — unresearched.
- Black women keypunch / data-processing operators — no Black-specific verified case; general Smithsonian evidence on keypunch labor exists but is not race-specific.
- Black truck drivers, teamsters, longshoremen in Chicago — not covered.
- Ford Chicago Assembly (Torrence Avenue): Black-specific evidence filtered through harassment litigation; direct evidence of Black workers' assignments on specific systems is thin.

**Agenda:** Archival pass on Pullman Company, U.S. Steel, Western Electric/Hawthorne Works personnel and engineering records; Illinois Bell employment records; CTA historical records; railroad telegrapher rosters.

- Walter Lincoln Hawkins: exact patent number(s) for plastic cable-sheathing unidentified.

- Norbert Rillieux: possible earlier US patent (US 3237, 1843) unverified.
- Patricia Bath: US 4,721,359 grant date/title needs confirmation.
- Jesse Russell: digital cellular base-station patent numbers unconfirmed.
- Lloyd Quarterman: no personal patent identified; Met Lab/Argonne contributions lack institutional confirmation.
- Gerald Lawson: no personal patent identified; recorded as engineer/team leader pending patent searching.
- Marian Croak: US 7,599,359 title/date/co-inventors and US 7,715,368 text-to-donate association need confirmation.
- Otis Boykin: no specific US patent number found for the widely attributed heart-stimulator control unit — **flagged as the package's most important unresolved patent attribution.**
- Carver: grant dates for US 1,541,478 and US 1,632,365 conflict across secondary sources.

**Agenda:** Direct USPTO/Google Patents verification pass for all Provisional records before any public "patented by" language.

- Chess lacquer-cutting engineers unnamed in sources found ("Sheldon prepared the lacquers"; RCA-Indianapolis cut others).

## Zone 4 — Digital-era primary evidence (the thin middle)

- No verified "first Black website" or "first Black person online" — treated as Unresolved; any public claim must be suppressed until primary evidence exists.
- Dates for Universal Black Pages, BlackVoices founding, BDPA not directly verified from primary sources.
- No verified Black-founded mobile-app or mobile-hardware company identified this pass; mobile rests on component inventors.
- Chicago digital-production specifics beyond No I.D./Kanye/Knuckles are thin — DJ Rashad, RP Boo (footwork), and house producers' DAW-era practices unresearched.
- Game developers beyond Lawson need verification; Spawn On Me launch year unverified.
- Omar Wasow's BlackPlanet co-founder(s) not verified — do not name.
- Early-web artifacts (AfroNet, NetNoir, Popandpolitics) need Wayback/archival confirmation of dates.

**Agenda:** Internet Archive mining; oral histories with footwork producers and early Black web publishers; studio-engineer interviews.

## Zone 5 — AI: claims outrunning evidence

- No verified census of Black AI researchers, engineers, or lab leads at Chicago institutions.
- No peer-reviewed study found measuring how LLM training data misrepresents Black history/culture specifically — bias is documented at the facial-recognition level (Gender Shades), not the knowledge-representation level.
- No verified Black-community data trust, cooperative, or consent framework for AI training data — only positions exist.
- Latimer LLM (FutureSum AI): public status, architecture, and training-data sourcing unverified as of 2026-09-28 (Unresolved).
- Black in AI's 2024–2026 membership scale and program status not verified.
- DAIR exact launch date; "Stochastic Parrots" title/timeline; Algorithmic Justice League scope — Provisional.
- Effects of LLM training data on Black history/culture absent independent audit — deliberately left unresolved.

**Agenda:** Re-grade pass in 12 months; monitor Latimer LLM and data-trust proposals; commission or locate audit research on LLM knowledge representation of Black culture.
