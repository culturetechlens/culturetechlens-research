## 1. Black Memory Emergency carousel (10 slides)

Source: *The Black Memory Emergency: What Happens When Black History Disappears*,
William Maxey, CultureTechLens, October 2026. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23173585 (CC BY 4.0).
All stats verbatim from the paper. Post as a 10-slide Instagram/Threads/Facebook carousel.

| File | Slide | Suggested caption |
|---|---|---|
| `carousel-memory-emergency-01.png` | Hook — THE BLACK MEMORY EMERGENCY | "Black cultural memory in America isn't disappearing because nobody recorded it. It's disappearing because of everything that happens *after*. A ten-slide story. 🧵" |
| `carousel-memory-emergency-02.png` | The research — 84 collections, 304 sources | "We mapped 84 collections, 119 entities, 70 timeline events, and 304 sources to find where the chain breaks. (2/10)" |
| `carousel-memory-emergency-03.png` | Destroyer 01 — cataloging backlogs | "The AFRO archive: ~3 million photographs, 130 years of a family-owned Black press — about 5% processed since 2022. Saved and simultaneously lost. (3/10)" |
| `carousel-memory-emergency-04.png` | Destroyer 02 — ownership change | "The Ebony/Jet archive — 4 million+ images — reached a bankruptcy auction in July 2019. Four foundations, two days, just under $30 million. The rescue is the exception that maps the rule. (4/10)" |
| `carousel-memory-emergency-05.png` | Destroyer 03 — collector mortality | "In 2025, a photographer's life's-work negatives burned in a friend's garage during a move. Teenie Harris's ~80,000 negatives survived because one museum bought them in 2001. (5/10)" |
| `carousel-memory-emergency-06.png` | Destroyer 04 — rights uncertainty | "Eyes on the Prize vanished for roughly a decade over expired music licenses. $850,000+ to bring it back. Rights is the blocker that operates after every physical problem is solved. (6/10)" |
| `carousel-memory-emergency-07.png` | Destroyer 05 — platform deletion | "BET Digital deleted its entire written news archive in September 2026. No warning. MySpace lost ~50M songs. Platform memory is not memory. (7/10)" |
| `carousel-memory-emergency-08.png` | The ten-stage audit | "CATALOGED: worst-supported stage. DISCOVERABLE: thinnest. Black memory is created and collected at scale — then enters a middle passage where most loss occurs. (8/10)" |
| `carousel-memory-emergency-09.png` | The response — triage framework | "Our answer: a transparent triage framework — Risk × Significance × Uniqueness, gated by Feasibility. A red-team audit forced 23 claim downgrades. Honesty is the method. (9/10)" |
