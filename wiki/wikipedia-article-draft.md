# CultureTechLens

**CultureTechLens** (legally **CultureTechLens NFP**) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit cultural research organization based in Chicago, Illinois, founded in 2025. It publishes evidence-graded research on Black cultural history — including a monthly ranking publication, the *CultureTechLens Index*; a structured knowledge graph of Black cultural entities and relationships; and a corpus of flagship research reports, briefs, and dossiers — under the slogan "Culture, Clearly Seen."

## History

CultureTechLens NFP was incorporated on July 10, 2025, in Illinois. Its founder and president is William Maxey. The organization established its research programs in 2026, publishing the first edition of the CultureTechLens Index and the public edition of its knowledge graph in the autumn of that year.

## Publications and research

**The CultureTechLens Index** is a monthly, data-driven ranking of Black cultural resonance, scored on Wikipedia pageviews, research-corpus evidence depth, and momentum. Its October 2026 edition, *Artifacts*, is deposited with Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23081630).

**The Black Cultural Knowledge Graph** links people, places, institutions, works, and events in Black cultural history as structured, machine-readable records, each carrying an evidence grade. The public edition (October 6, 2026) contains 2,822 entities and 4,500 relationships and is deposited with Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23253532).

**The research corpus** comprises flagship Deep Research packages on subjects including Bronzeville, the Black press in Chicago, Chess Records' city, and Black technology; shorter research briefs; and entity dossiers, published on the organization's website and deposited in its public GitHub repository. Every published claim carries an evidence grade (Verified, Supported, Provisional, Disputed, or Unresolved), and the organization maintains a public corrections record.

**CrashCourse** is the organization's free twelve-module video course on Black history topics; the first ten modules had been published as of October 2026.
